## Overview

Version 4.3.2 introduces a refined whip base inspired by car-antenna mechanics, combined with the established Gali-Spinal Tube architecture (TiC + PTFE tube, magnetite sphere chain, double-headed ferrite core, pure iron return path). The goal is improved mechanical resonance, better external centroid stability, and enhanced twist-force coupling.

---

## Physical Construction

### Primary Tube

- Outer structural layer: Titanium carbide (TiC) for high strength and thermal stability
- Inner lining: PTFE for chemical inertness, low friction, and compatibility with carrier fluids
- Internal working volume: Chain of highly purified magnetized magnetite spheres (2–8 mm) suspended in carrier fluid (deionized/dark water or Galinstan variant)

### Whip Base (Car-Antenna Model)

- Base material options: Nitinol (superelastic) or 316L stainless steel
- Microchannel structure for Galinstan infusion (improved magnetic and thermal coupling)
- Designed for cantilever resonance in the target RPM range
- Unbalanced spiny whip head at the distal end to drive the sphere chain

### Core and Return Path

- Double-headed sliced ferrite starburst core
- Pure iron magnetic return path enclosing the tube
- Designed to maintain strong localized field while supporting external centroid stability
