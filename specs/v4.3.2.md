## 1. Overview

The Gali-Spinal Tube is a vertical, sealed magneto-sonic resonance column combining advanced materials, liquid-metal carrier fluid, size-scaled magnetite resonators, two stabilized micro-bubble molecules, a piezoelectric healing amplifier, and a **Galinstan-infused car-antenna-style whip base**.

It produces aggressive high-frequency ionizing output (Energy Head) and gentle restorative low-intensity pulsed ultrasound (Molecular Healing Head).

**Intended applications:** advanced sonochemical processing, plasma-physics studies, therapeutic ultrasound research (LIPUS-inspired), and biomedical magneto-sonic reactors.

Matte metallic finish with subtle purple Galinstan accent lines. Mounts like a car-antenna boot to the rigid TiC/PTFE tube.
