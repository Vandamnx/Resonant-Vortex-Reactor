## One-line description

A lattice sphere **spins around a stationary central hub** fixed to the spinal column. Channels carry **40% DMSO + Galinstan**. Magnetite spheres ride the wrap and **lock harmoniously** as the head spins. Magnetic return closes through the sphere, N to S. Whip action of the springy steel antenna spine sends spheres north.

## Geometry

### Head + stationary hub

- Spherical lattice / cage that **rotates**
- **Stationary central hub** on the axis, fixed to the spinal column (stator)
- Globe = rotor. Hub + spine do not spin with it
- Helical or multi-start microchannels on the spinning surface
- Carrier: 40% DMSO + Galinstan (water / sap variants are side branches)
- Magnetite spheres 2–8 mm class
- As the head spins, spheres **lock harmoniously** (stable spacing / phase)
- Orange ionizing nodes, purple de-ionizing nodes
- Micro-bubble sites in channels or node pockets

### Interior

- **Loop return path** inside the globe (not only the outer helix)
- Hub shown as center boss (four-dot fastener / bearing)
- **Offset steel reinforcement** off-axis for extra whip
- Unbalance force: F = m e ω²

### Poles

- N at the crown, S at the stem junction
