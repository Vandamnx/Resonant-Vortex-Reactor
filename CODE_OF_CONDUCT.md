# Code of conduct

This is an open experimental-engineering project. Be useful. Be civil. Don’t turn the issues tab into a church or a fight club.

## Expected

- Critique the design and the physics. Bring numbers, citations, or a build note.
- Label work the same way the repo does: **Fact** / **Design** / **Vision**.
- Assume good faith. The sketches are real work even when the claims are unproven.
- Keep discussion about the machine, the tests, and the docs.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or stalking of the maintainer or anyone else.
- Dumping medical advice or “this device heals / cures” as if it were a result.
- Presenting Vision (Time Particle Theory, field-as-organ, etc.) as measured Fact.
- Over-unity / free-energy marketing copy in PRs or issues.
- Scraping or republishing notebooks as your invention.
- Security noise: don’t paste secrets, API keys, or someone else’s private data.

## Project facts you agree to when you participate

- Not a finished energy product.
- Not a medical device.
- Not a demonstration of over-unity.
- Time Particle Theory is labeled speculation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (Vandamnx) can edit, close, or ban. No drama required. If something is unsafe or off-scope, it goes.

## If you want to contribute

Open an issue first for anything bigger than a typo. Bench results beat slogans.
