## 1. Core Premise

Time is not a smooth, continuous river. It is composed of discrete, fundamental **rigid-body instants** — Planck-scale “now” moments that possess structure and can be influenced, twisted, phase-shifted, or modulated.

These instants are not passive background. They form the substrate of experienced reality and can be actively engaged through specific configurations of force, rhythm, rotation, and coherence.

**Time Particle Theory** proposes that certain physical systems — particularly those generating sustained **twist-force standing waves** while maintaining a stable **external centroid of rotation** — can couple to and modulate these time particles, altering the local texture, duration, or phase relationships of instants.

## 2. Fundamental Principles

### 2.1 Rigid-Body Instants

- Time consists of discrete Planck-scale instants rather than a continuous flow.
- Each instant has a definable “thickness” or duration that can be modulated.
- These instants can be phase-shifted, stretched, compressed, or twisted relative to one another.

### 2.2 Twist-Force Standing Waves

- The primary mechanism for interacting with time particles.
- Generated by rhythmic, unbalanced rotational or oscillatory systems.
- When these waves reach sufficient intensity and coherence (e.g., “sonic threshold snap”), they can meaningfully influence the structure or relationships of rigid-body instants.

### 2.3 External Centroid of Rotation

- A stable external reference point around which internal motion occurs.
- Critical for coherent, sustained coupling to time particles.
- When the external centroid remains stable, twist-force can accumulate and affect instants. When it wobbles or decoheres, coupling collapses.

### 2.4 Neutrino as the “Unseeable State”

- Under strong twist-force conditions, certain states or particles can transition into an effectively invisible or non-interacting mode relative to normal spacetime.
- Neutrinos are proposed as one expression of this “unseeable state” — slipping between modulated instants.

### 2.5 Dual Polarity of Time Modulation

- **Time-Forward / Expansive Phase** (Light-Force Expansion): Associated with ionizing, power-generating, outward-directed dynamics.
- **Time-Reversal / Restorative Phase** (Heart-Action Analog): Associated with de-ionizing, coherence-restoring, inward-directed dynamics.
- These two polarities exist in rhythmic relationship, analogous to the cosmic egg (contraction ↔ expansion) and the heart’s action potential cycle.

### 2.6 Planck-Scale Wave Duration

- The duration or “thickness” of individual rigid-body instants can be influenced by sufficiently coherent twist-force standing waves.
- This modulation may become detectable at high harmonic frequencies where standing waves intensify (MHz-range “song/hum” building to sonic threshold events).
